Ken U Diggit Content System - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Ken U Diggit Content System ("the App") is a private tool operated by the electronic music artist Ken U Diggit to prepare and publish the artist's own music videos and to read the performance statistics of the artist's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

Who can use the App

The App is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the App accesses

With the owner's permission (via each platform's official sign-in), the App can upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, TikTok account and Instagram account, and read the view, like, comment and watch-time statistics of those same posts.

What data the App stores

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a restricted local folder. Statistics are stored in a local spreadsheet file. The App does not collect, store or process personal information about viewers, followers or any other third party.

Sharing and selling

The App does not sell, share, transfer or disclose any data to anyone. Data obtained from Google APIs is used only to provide the features described above, and the App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke the App's access at any time from the Google, TikTok or Meta account security settings, and can delete the local files at any time.
